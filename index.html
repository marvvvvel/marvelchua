Hello!
I'm Marvel

A marketing student at SUSS working where brand strategy meets storytelling, majoring in Marketing with a minor in Digital Content.

What I love most is building brands of my own, taking an idea from a name and a feeling to something people can actually see and connect with. That same energy goes into everything I work on, from brand identity to editorial design. These days, I tend to observe the things around me, from shopfronts to the way people talk about what they love. That’s where most of my ideas for my own brands start.

(B)  Branding · PSST
Brand Identity · Hot Sauce · 2026

PSST!

Your cooking's dirty little secret

The idea

Some Sauces Shout. PSST Whispers.

It leans in close, right by your ear, and tells you to add just a little more than you should. That little voice? That's us, the devil on your shoulder who happens to be a phenomenal cook, nudging you toward the bottle when no one's looking. The pan's hot, the meal's fine, and then comes the flicker of temptation: go on, give it some heat.

So we bottled that feeling and gave it horns, a grin, and a pitchfork, because the best cooking has always had a little devil in it. We're not the chef, we're the whisper behind the chef. Every plate that comes out dangerously delicious? That's us, taking none of the credit and all of the blame. Heat with a wink, mischief in a bottle.

BrekkieWithUs started at a breakfast table. A group of friends who never missed a weekend kopi and kaya toast decided to start reviewing the spots they kept coming back to. (C)  Campaigns · Fly To SUcceSS
School campaign · 2025

Fly To SUcceSS

SUSS Student SUcceSS Centre, Ngee Ann Block 23
Concept campaign made for a school assignment (SUSS MKT368).
Client
SUSS Student SUcceSS Centre
Role
Direction, content generation, trend research, shoot organisation, location scouting
Year
2025
Context
SUSS MKT368, school project
The brief

The Student Success Centre launched a new student space at Blk 23, near Nanyang Polytechnic, with a gym, a student wellness lounge, workshop and seminar rooms, and co-working spaces. Our team was tasked with a digital marketing strategy to get SUSS students to use them, led by a TikTok video.

Primary objective

To position the new Student SUcceSS Centre as a vibrant hub for student wellbeing, collaboration, and growth by promoting facilities:

  • Aiming to achieve 500 verified students visits tracked via the tap-in card system
  • 300 facility bookings through campaign links and QR codes

All targets to be achieved within 2 months of launch.

01 / Storyboard

Before the shoot, we planned the video in nine scenes, mapping each stop in the centre to what students can use there. The storyboard frames were generated with AI, which meant learning how to prompt for each scene and keep the characters consistent from frame to frame.

02 / TikTok video

A student flies through the new centre on a broomstick, from the gym on Level 1 to the Level 4 lift lobby, the Social Innovation Space, Project Room 2, Penny University Cafe and the co-working spaces, each stop calling out what students can use there. It ends on the call to action: “What are you waiting for? Come to Ngee Ann Block 23 today!” I directed the shoot on the day; the video was edited by my teammates.

Directed by Marvel Chua

Lab Grown, Best Grown

Singapore Food Agency × Huber's Butchery
Concept campaign made for a school assignment (SUSS MKT358). Not affiliated with or commissioned by Singapore Food Agency or Huber's Butchery.
Client
Singapore Food Agency
Role
Mockups, direction, video shoot
Year
2025
Context
SUSS MKT358, school project
The brief

The Singapore Food Agency tasked us with an advertising campaign to help Singaporeans accept lab-grown meat. Huber's Butchery was the first in the world to sell it on shelves, but most people still feel ambivalent about eating it.

The idea

Make lab-grown chicken feel like any normal chicken. Instead of explaining the science, the campaign lets local Singaporeans see it and taste it for themselves: if it looks like chicken and tastes like chicken, it is chicken.
